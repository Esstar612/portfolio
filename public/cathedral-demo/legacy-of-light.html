Legacy of Light

Ensuring the spiritual and architectural brilliance of our Cathedral for the centuries to come.

Preserving the Sacred Vessel

"The stones of this cathedral are held together not just by mortar, but by the faith of those who walked before us."

A bequest to the Cathedral of All Saints is more than a financial contribution; it is a spiritual anchor. Legacy gifts ensure that our Gothic arches, historic stained glass, and sacred spaces remain a refuge for all who seek peace, regardless of the era.

Your foresight allows us to maintain the delicate structural integrity of our historic home, funding critical restoration work that preserves the artistry of our ancestors.

A Voice for the Future

Music is the breath of the Cathedral. Legacy funds support our choral traditions, the maintenance of our magnificent organ, and the education of young choristers who will carry the liturgy forward.

Beyond our walls, bequests fuel our community outreach programs—feeding the hungry, supporting local arts, and providing a center for intellectual and spiritual gathering in the heart of the city.

Ways to Give

Types of Bequests

Residuary Bequest

A gift of the remainder of your estate after all other specific legacies, debts, and taxes have been paid. This maintains its value over time.

Most Impactful

Pecuniary Bequest

A gift of a fixed sum of money. While simple to specify, the value of these gifts can decrease over time due to inflation.

Simple & Direct

Specific Bequest

A gift of a specific item, such as property, shares, or works of art. These require prior consultation with our legal team.

Tangible Legacy

Standard Wording for Your Will

"I give to the Cathedral of All Saints (Registered Charity No. XXXXXX) the sum of $______ (or _____% of my residuary estate) to be used for its general charitable purposes, and I declare that the receipt of the Treasurer or other proper officer shall be a full and sufficient discharge to my executors."

A Confidential Conversation

Making a gift in your will is a deeply personal decision. If you would like to discuss how your legacy could specifically impact the Cathedral, or if you wish to notify us of a gift already made, we would be honored to speak with you.
