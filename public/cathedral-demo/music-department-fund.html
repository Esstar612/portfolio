"The music of the Cathedral is the breath of its spiritual life, carrying our prayers to the heavens on the wings of the Anglican choral tradition."

For over a century, the Cathedral of All Saints has served as a beacon of liturgical excellence. Our music program, anchored by the historic pipe organ and our dedicated choir, remains a cornerstone of the spiritual experience for thousands each year.

Maintaining such a legacy requires more than just passion; it demands resources to sustain the delicate restoration of our historic instruments and the rigorous training of the next generation of choral scholars. Your generosity ensures that these sacred sounds continue to echo through our hallowed halls for generations to come.

By contributing to the Music Department Fund, you directly support organ maintenance, music scores, vocal training, and the high-fidelity streaming of our services to a global audience.
