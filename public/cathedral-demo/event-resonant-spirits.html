Featured Gathering

ResonantSpirits

An evening of sacred choral masterpieces under the vaulted ceiling of the Great Nave. Join our resident choir for a journey through the ages of liturgical sound.

"In the architecture of silence, we find the resonance of our own souls. Every gathering at All Saints is an invitation to listen to the eternal."

Date
Saturday, April 18
Time
7:00 — 9:00 PM
Location
The Great Nave
Admission
Free / Donation

About this Event

Resonant Spirits is the Cathedral's signature evening of sacred choral music, returning this April for its tenth season. The Cathedral Choir, joined by the Schola Cantorum and our Resident Organist, will lead a journey through five centuries of liturgical composition — from the luminous polyphony of Palestrina and Byrd to contemporary works by Eriks Ešenvalds and Caroline Shaw.

The performance unfolds in the Great Nave under candlelight, with the choir processing through the historic aisles. Audience members are invited to arrive at 6:30 PM for a short pre-concert reflection by Dean Henderson, who will speak briefly about the spiritual context of the evening's repertoire.

This is one of the most beloved gatherings in the Cathedral's annual calendar, and seating is unreserved. We encourage early arrival to find a spot that suits your preferred listening experience.

Before You Arrive

Practical Information

Parking & Transit

Limited parking on Cathedral Plaza. The 30 and 41 buses stop at our front gate. Ample street parking on Saturday evenings.

Accessibility

The Nave is fully wheelchair accessible via the West Door. Hearing-loop seating is available in rows 4–8. Service animals welcome.

What to Wear

Smart casual is welcome. The Nave can be cool in the evening — a light jacket or shawl is recommended.
