Friends of the
Choir

Sustaining a tradition of excellence and spiritual depth through the power of choral music.

"The music of the Cathedral is not merely a performance; it is a vital part of our liturgy, lifting hearts toward the divine and preserving centuries of sacred heritage."

The Cathedral Music Department provides a home for some of the finest choral traditions in the region. By joining the Friends of the Choir, you ensure that this legacy of beauty and devotion continues to flourish for generations to come.
