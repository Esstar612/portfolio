Planned Giving

Ensuring the light of the Cathedral continues to shine for generations to come through intentional legacy stewardship.

A Legacy of Faith

"For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also."

Since our founding, the Cathedral of All Saints has been sustained by the generosity of those who looked beyond their own lifetimes. Planned giving offers a way to make a significant and lasting contribution to the Cathedral's mission and ministry.

By including the Cathedral in your estate plans, you ensure that this digital and physical sanctuary remains a beacon of hope, art, and spirituality for future generations.

Ways to Give

Stewardship Options

Bequests

A gift made through your will or trust is the most common way to support the Cathedral. It allows you to retain use of your assets during your lifetime.

Learn More

Appreciated Securities

Donating long-term appreciated stock or mutual funds can provide significant tax advantages while supporting our endowment and programs.

Transfer Instructions

Gift Annuities

Charitable gift annuities provide you with a fixed income for life in exchange for your generous contribution to the Cathedral's future.

The Heritage Society

The Heritage Society recognizes and honors individuals who have expressed their commitment to the Cathedral of All Saints by naming the Cathedral as a beneficiary in their estate plans.

  • Annual appreciation luncheon with the Dean and Clergy.
  • Special recognition in the Cathedral's annual report.
  • Permanent listing in the Heritage Society Registry.
